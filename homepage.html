    Introduction to Betta Fish

    Betta fish, also known as Siamese fighting fish, are vibrant freshwater fish native to Southeast Asia...

    Types of Betta Fish

    • Veil Tail – Traditional long, flowing tail.
    • Halfmoon – Tail spreads 180°, resembling a half circle.
    • Crowntail – Tail rays extend outward, giving a spiky appearance.
    • Plakat – Short-finned, resembling wild-type bettas.
    • Delta – Tail shaped like the Greek letter Δ.
    • Super Delta – Tail angle slightly greater than Delta.
    • Double Tail – Two caudal fins instead of one.

    Color Patterns

    • Solid – One uniform color.
    • Butterfly – Solid body with lighter-colored fins.
    • Dragon Scale – Metallic sheen with scale-like patterns.

    Care Tips

    • Maintain water temperature between 76°F and 81°F.
    • Provide a tank with a lid to prevent jumping.
    • Feed a balanced diet of high-quality pellets and occasional live food.
    • Avoid keeping multiple males together to prevent aggression.
